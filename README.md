# My Digital Translator

My Digital Translator is a hexagonal architecture + LLM engineering demo 
tool that combines web scraping with an LLM model. 
Its aim is to act as an assistant for autistic individuals that removes 
overwhelming contents from a website and keeps just relevant ones, 
adapting them to a clear, predictable and easy to read output.

## Example use cases
### Simplifying chaotic digital environments
Webpages are full of ads, complex menus and links that can cause sensory overload. A scrapper can extract the meaningful content and the LLM can rewrite it removing ambiguous language, distractive visual elements, returning it using a structured and predictable format.

### Removing ambiguous or implicit language
The LLM is configured to translate content into a direct, neutral and literal language.

### Planning and anticipating events
Sudden changes in routine, uncertainty over a new environment and the logistics of a trip can cause high stress levels. This tool can extract information of public places and organize it into a chronological, step-by-step guide to ensure the user can anticipate the trip.

### Filtering news and alarmist information
The system is designed to summarize news in an objective way to avoid alarming titles and polarized opinions.

### Assisting social interaction in the web
Autistic individuals may find trouble understanding social rules and expectations of a new site, such as a forum or work email. The system is designed to analyze the norms of a site and summarize them into a clear output of what to do and what not to do.
